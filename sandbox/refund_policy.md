# Harbourline Payments: refund policy

Applies to hardware and supplies ordered through the merchant portal.
All amounts are in the platform currency. All dates are UTC.

1. **Never more than was paid.** A refund cannot exceed the order amount minus anything
   already refunded. An order refunded in full gets no further refund.
2. **Charged twice.** If the order shows two or more charges of the same amount, one charge
   is refunded in full. If it shows only one charge, the request is declined.
3. **Never arrived.** If no delivery is recorded and at least 3 days have passed since the
   expected delivery date, the order is refunded in full. If a delivery is recorded, or the
   order is not yet 3 days late, the request is declined.
4. **Arrived damaged.** Refunded in full if the customer sends a photo and asks within 30 days
   of delivery. Without a photo the request is declined and the customer is asked for one.
5. **Changed their mind.** Refunded in full if the customer asks within 14 days of delivery;
   declined after that.
6. **Anything else** (faults, partial problems, unclear messages) goes to a support specialist.

**Who decides.** Refunds of 50 or less that meet this policy are paid straight away. Larger
refunds that meet this policy are paid once a support specialist approves them. A refund
paid in error can be cancelled within 24 hours, before it settles.
